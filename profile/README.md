# VIET CodeHub

### Visakha Institute of Engineering & Technology (A)

Welcome to **VIET CodeHub** — a collaborative platform for software development, technology, innovation, and open-source initiatives at **Visakha Institute of Engineering & Technology (A)**.

We bring together students, faculty, and developers to **build, learn, collaborate, and innovate** through practical software projects and technology initiatives.

* 💻 Software Development & Engineering
* 🎓 Student & Academic Projects
* 👨‍🏫 Faculty Technology Initiatives
* 🏆 Hackathons & Coding Events

* 🤖 AI & Emerging Technologies
* ☁️ Cloud & Modern Web Technologies
* 🌱 Open-Source Development
* 📚 Developer Resources & Documentation
